What I believe

The future belongs to those who believe in potential, in people who have not yet been given the right conditions, and in resources we have not yet respected enough to use well. I believe in unlocking what is already there. And I believe that a good adventure is the mix of discovery and accepting discomfort.

The three things I bring

Potential.
Respect.
Enjoyment.

These are not values written on a wall. They are my filter for running every project, every team, every decision through. When all three are present, organisations move. When one is missing, I ask why before I do anything else.

01

Potential

People are almost always more capable than the system around them allows. Resources are almost always more valuable than the way they are being used. My job is to close that gap — in operations, in teams, in organisations going through change.

02

Respect

Respect for the person on the floor and the one in the boardroom. Respect for the environment as a stakeholder, not a constraint. Respect for complexity, not pretending things are simpler than they are to make yourself look decisive.

03

Enjoyment

Work that does not have some element of genuine engagement is work that will not be done well. I have climbed mountains, managed a surf camp, and rowed to two world championship medals. I bring that same energy to organisations that are willing to earn it.
